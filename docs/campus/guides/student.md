# Student guide

## Your courses

- **Dashboard** lists your courses, what's due and recent notifications.
- Each course opens on the home page your teacher chose: modules, a front page, the syllabus, assignments, or recent activity.
- Locked items say why (an earlier module, a date, a requirement).

## Assignments and quizzes

- Submit text, links or files (Word, Excel, PDF, notebooks, `.py` and similar). Files are scanned before anyone can open them.
- Late work may lose points per day or per hour as your course's late policy says; missing work can receive a set score.
- Some quizzes have a calculator beside the questions, an access code, or must be taken from an approved location (for example a campus lab).
- **Proctored quizzes:** complete the pre-test checklist first. If your school uses a proctoring tool, press **Start proctoring**; the tool checks your setup and sends you back to start.

## Grades

- Grades appear after your teacher posts them. You'll see grade points when your course uses a GPA scheme, and Pass/Fail for pass/fail courses.
- Rubric feedback shows each criterion's rating and comments.
- GPA shown in the campus is for courses in progress — it is not an official transcript.

## Pages and discussions

- If your teacher allows it, you can edit some pages (the body only). Every save is kept.
- Your course decides whether you can edit or delete your own discussion posts.

## Groups and portfolio

- Groups you belong to have shared **group files** with a storage limit.
- Your **portfolio** is private. Turn on its public link to share it; turning it off stops the old link from working. The public page shows only what you add — never grades or comments.

## Getting an account

If your school allows it, use **Request an account** on the sign-in pages. An administrator approves each request.
