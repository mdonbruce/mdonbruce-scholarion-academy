Scholarion SBL framework

Every course is built around doing the work.

Every weekly topic includes a scenario, Mini Lab, scenario-based knowledge check, Mini Project and a milestone that contributes to a cumulative real-world Capstone.

Scenario→ Learn→ Practice→ Mini Lab→ Scenario Quiz→ Mini Project→ Project Milestone→ Feedback→ Capstone→ Mastery
