Questions learners ask

Frequently asked questions.

Are these degree programs?

No. Scholaris AI Academy offers certificate-of-completion and professional-certificate programs. The site does not claim degree or accredited status unless a formal approval exists.

How much time should I plan each week?

Builder programs target about 6–10 hours per week. No-Code, Business & Leadership, Business & Product, and Industry programs generally target about 4–6 hours per week.

What do I need to complete a program?

Completion requires at least 70% overall, all labs submitted, a passing capstone, and at least 80% participation in live/mentored components or an approved asynchronous alternative.

Do I need programming experience?

Builder-track programs require Python. No-Code programs do not require coding. Business and Industry programs range from none to light or guided coding.

How is AI used responsibly?

Every program includes privacy, security, human oversight, evaluation, guardrails, auditability, and bias/fairness content appropriate to the audience and domain.

Can I stack one certificate into another?

Yes, selected shorter programs may transfer into longer pathways. Transferable modules and prior-learning rules are defined program by program.
