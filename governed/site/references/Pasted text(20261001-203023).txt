## 1. RULES

1. **Market references are internal only.** The references are a commercial training provider's agentic AI engineering course, its AI category page and its related AI certificate courses. Never reuse their:
   - Titles, module or topic wording, project names, FAQ text
   - Testimonials, learner counts, ratings or review-site scores
   - Market and salary statistics, partner logos, pricing or refund terms
2. **Statistics.** Market, salary or adoption figures appear only from a **current, cited primary source**, verified and dated at publish time. Learner counts, ratings and testimonials come only from real Scholaris data, with consent. Otherwise omit them.
3. **Credentials.** Scholaris AI Academy credentials only:
   - Certificate of Completion
   - Graded Performance Certificate (optional)
   - Open Badges 3.0

   Never use "PG", "Masters", "degree", "accredited" or "industry-recognized" wording unless a real approval exists.
4. **No job guarantees.** Career services are described as services, never outcomes.
5. **Tools.** Verify each tool is current and educationally licensed, pin versions in the Scholaris Cloud Lab, name tools in plain text, and include a no-affiliation disclaimer.
6. **Sandbox.** Payments, installments and "enroll now, pay later" run in sandbox until go-live. API keys are Scholaris-issued, spend-capped and expiring.
7. **Data.** All datasets are synthetic or licensed, each with a data card.
8. **Textbook alignment.**
   - Python foundations modules reference **Gaddis, *Starting Out with Python*, 6th ed.**
   - ML/DL modules may reference **Raschka et al., *Machine Learning with PyTorch and Scikit-Learn*** and **Géron, *Hands-On Machine Learning*, 3rd ed.** as recommended readings, with chapter mapping in the instructor guide.

## 5. PROGRAM #15 — AGENTIC AI ENGINEERING WEEKEND INTENSIVE (detailed spec)

### 5.1 Key facts
- **Format:** 10 weekends, live online. Saturday and Sunday, 3 hours each (60 live hours), plus ~4–6 hours/week of self-paced labs.
- **Batches:** rolling cohorts every 4–6 weeks with time-zone-specific batches. Cohort size capped (admin-set).
- **Prerequisites:** working Python and comfort calling APIs. No prior agent framework or Docker experience required.
- **Pedagogy:** about 70% hands-on (demo → lab → checkpoint every session).
- **Completion requires:**
  - 20 live modules (attended, or recording reviewed plus lab submitted)
  - All module assessments passed
  - Capstone scoring at least 70/100 on the mentor rubric, with a live demo defense
  - Final knowledge check passed (2 attempts; free remediation between attempts)
- **Credentials:** Certificate of Completion, Graded Performance Certificate and capstone badge, all verifiable by ID.
