Business & Product · Program #12

Certificate in Designing & Building AI Products and Services

Design, validate, and pitch responsible AI products and services by connecting product strategy, machine learning, generative AI, agents, human–AI interaction, economics, and implementation planning.

Length

Credential

Program design

What you will learn

  • Classify ML approaches and neural architectures and match them to product problems.
  • Apply an AI product design process from problem framing through validation, including data, cost, and technical requirements.
  • Evaluate generative AI and agent capabilities—including RAG, reasoning prompts, tool use, and MCP—for product fit and risk.
  • Design human–AI interaction and collective-intelligence models with appropriate automation and oversight.
  • Build and pitch a business case covering cost–benefit, strategic alignment, risk, and an implementation roadmap.

Curriculum

Week-by-week learning path

WeekFocus
1AI product design process: problem framing, stages, cost metrics and technical requirements
2Machine learning for product people: classifiers, train/validate/test, Bayesian and regression models, clustering; optional no-code lab
3Deep learning essentials: neurons, gradient descent, MLPs, CNNs, RNNs and autoencoders; cited public case studies
4Transformers and generative AI: tokens, embeddings, decoders, capabilities and failure modes; responsible synthetic media
5GenAI in products: prompting, benchmarks, RAG and reasoning prompts; no-code RAG assistant assignment
6Agentic AI landscape: agents, tool use, MCP, interoperability standards and agent product patterns
7Human–AI interaction design: automation levels, trust, transparency, error recovery and accessibility
8Human + machine collective intelligence: designing teams and organizations that combine people and AI
9AI product economics, governance, risk, validation and implementation planning
10Product proposal studio, stakeholder pitch and final presentation
