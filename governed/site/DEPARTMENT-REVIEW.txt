Hold rules require policy.finance.holds with advance_notice_seconds, reminder_days, levels [{name:soft|hard,after_days}]. Late processing announces before enforcement and delays effective time by the notice interval. Notices include outstanding amount and clearance instructions. A zero outstanding balance yields release intents immediately. Partial payment does not clear an outstanding balance. Intents require a future transactional worker and idempotency store; no holds are currently executed.
Refund rules require policy.finance.refunds with auto_approve_limit_minor and ascending schedule [{through_day,refund_basis_points}]. Pay-as-you-go scope is course; full/installment scope is program. Calculations use collected amounts minus prior refunds, in integer minor units, and route out-of-schedule or over-limit results to a review decision. No refund processor or staff review queue is connected. Currency, policy anchor date, installment liabilities and fee treatment need Part E before execution.
Identity rules require policy.identity.provider {name,mode:sandbox} and policy.identity.retries {max_attempts}. Allowlisted result metadata only; unexpected fields rejected. Failed results omit name and DOB. Verified results require verified name/date of birth, result, provider reference and timestamp. This validator has no public callback endpoint and does not prove identity. No ID upload endpoint was created. Future provider signature, session ownership, freshness and replay checks are required before accepting provider events.
