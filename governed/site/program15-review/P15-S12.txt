P15-S12 — Use memory with evidence and expiry
Faculty-review draft.
Outcome: Separate durable facts from uncertain working notes.
Lab: Implement a research notebook with source references, confidence and expiry fields.
Assessment: Expired or unsupported memories cannot become verified claims.
Evidence: Implementation or design artifact, Test evidence with environment details, Limitations and remediation notes
Make-up: Review the approved recording and submit the session lab. Faculty validates equivalent participation.
