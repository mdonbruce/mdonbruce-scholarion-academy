P15-S04 — Measure retrieval before generation
Faculty-review draft.
Outcome: Evaluate lexical and semantic retrieval independently of answer style.
Lab: Chunk a synthetic catalog, define relevance labels and compare retrieval candidates.
Assessment: Calculate recall at k from the labeled set and explain a missed relevant record.
Evidence: Implementation or design artifact, Test evidence with environment details, Limitations and remediation notes
